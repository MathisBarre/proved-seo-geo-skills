# Scope and non-goals

## In scope

- Google Search, including Google AI features where Google documents their eligibility.
- Bing search and Bing's documented Copilot / grounding reporting.
- IndexNow only as a Bing-adjacent URL-change notification protocol.
- Technical site work: links, crawl access, robots, indexing controls, canonical URLs, sitemaps, rendering, markup, and media discoverability.
- Content and editorial experience: product/landing pages, site pages, articles, documentation, release notes, images, videos, and interactive experiences.
- Evidence-aware measurement and experiments.

## Out of scope

- Other search engines and assistants until separately curated.
- Guaranteed rankings, citations, traffic, conversions, or “AI visibility scores.”
- Spam, cloaking, prompt injection, content farms, deceptive competitor claims, or fabricated research.
- Legal, accessibility, security, paid-media, analytics implementation, or conversion-rate advice unless it is explicitly supported by a relevant source and scoped as such.
- A universal content template. The right form follows the visitor task and the evidence available.

## Terminology

This project uses **content and editorial experience** rather than “copywriting” when it includes text, images, video, data, embeds, or a mini-app. **GEO/GIO** is shorthand for making content suitable to be discovered and potentially reused in generative/AI search; it does not name a separate engine ranking system.
