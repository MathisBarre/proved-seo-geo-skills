# Philosophy

Search advice is useful only when it explains both its evidence and its uncertainty. This repository therefore optimizes for durable, inspectable decisions rather than folklore, “GEO hacks,” or claims that an agent cannot verify.

## The operating belief

Google Search and Bing can only retrieve, understand, and reuse information that is available to their systems under the conditions they document. A strong SEO foundation consequently also improves a page's opportunity to participate in AI-powered search experiences. Opportunity is not a guarantee of ranking, citation, mention, traffic, or revenue.

The practical priority is:

1. Help a real visitor accomplish something.
2. Make the essential facts public, accurate, traceable, and technically reachable.
3. Use the formats that genuinely help: text, image, video, data, or an interactive tool.
4. Measure the intended outcome separately from adjacent outcomes.

## What this repository refuses to sell

It does not claim a hidden Google or Bing ranking recipe. It does not translate a correlation into causation. It does not prescribe arbitrary counts of words, H1s, paragraphs, images, videos, FAQs, or schema fields.

It also does not equate a citation with success. A page can be cited without generating a visit, named without a citation, ranked without appearing in an AI surface, or commercially effective without any of those outcomes.

## Why evidence is layered

Primary engine documentation defines requirements, stated recommendations, and explicit non-factors. Secondary studies identify patterns worth testing. Local experiments establish only what happened in their recorded context. A heuristic is a decision aid, not a factual claim.

That layering lets an agent say “Google documents this,” “this study observed this,” or “this is a test proposal” instead of flattening all advice into one false certainty.
