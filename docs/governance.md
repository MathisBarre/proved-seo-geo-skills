# Evidence governance

The detailed rules used by the skill are [bundled with it](../skills/proved-seo-geo/references/evidence-policy.md). This page is the public contribution contract.

## Evidence tiers

| Tier | What it can support | What it cannot support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | An engine's stated requirement, recommendation, eligibility rule, or explicit rejection | A promise that the result will occur |
| Secondary observation | A bounded pattern worth testing | A universal ranking or citation factor |
| Experiment | A result in the documented site, market, period, and test design | A result for another context by default |
| Heuristic | A reversible local choice | A factual SEO claim |

## Admission test

A proposed claim needs all of the following:

1. A stable source URL and source record.
2. The exact claim, rewritten no more broadly than the evidence permits.
3. The evidence tier and scope.
4. Material limitations, confounders, or contradictions.
5. A way to verify the recommendation on a real site or content asset.

## Rejection test

Reject or quarantine a claim when it relies only on repetition, cannot name a method, mistakes a dashboard metric for a cause, implies certainty from a correlation, or tells users to manipulate an AI system rather than improve information for people.

New secondary domains start as candidates. They are not added to the approved registry until a reviewer can assess their author, method, sample, date, incentives, reproducibility, and relationship to the claim.
