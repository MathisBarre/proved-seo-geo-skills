# Content and editorial experience

Use this reference for landing pages, site pages, articles, documentation, release notes, and media. Source IDs resolve in [source-registry.json](source-registry.json).

## Common standard

Create information that is useful and reliable for people first: original analysis, experience, research, or explanation where the page can genuinely provide it. Make important claims explicit, accurate, and attributable when readers would expect attribution. Google and Bing both emphasize useful, clear, original content rather than search-engine-first repetition. `[G-HELPFUL] [G-AI-OPTIMIZATION] [B-GUIDELINES]`

Use descriptive titles and headings to orient a reader. Organize sections, lists, tables, FAQs, media, or a demo only when that format makes the answer clearer. No official source establishes a mandatory amount of content or a universal format.

## Landing and product pages

For a visitor making a decision, state the proposition, audience, capabilities, operating constraints, evidence, and next step in public page content. If a decisive claim appears in a screenshot, product tour, video, or live demo, also make a concise factual version readable on the page.

Describe comparison, pricing, availability, privacy, integration, performance, or limitation claims only when the business can support and maintain them. This is a practical application of useful, transparent content—not a promise that a landing page will be cited. `[G-HELPFUL] [B-GUIDELINES]`

## Articles and documentation

Aim to answer a bounded visitor task with facts, reasoning, original observations, or a plainly attributed synthesis. Prefer a real contribution over a generic restatement. For analytical or high-trust topics, identify the author/editor and cite the underlying materials where that helps readers evaluate the work. `[G-HELPFUL] [G-AI-OPTIMIZATION]`

Secondary research may motivate a test. For example, studies in the registry observe relationships among clarity, original data, or AI citations, but they do not establish a target word count, a mandatory FAQ, or a citation formula. `[S-SEM-CLARITY] [S-AHREFS-AIO-RANKS] [S-GROWTH-MEMO-ORIGINAL-DATA]`

## Release notes and version announcements

Write the version or release date, the exact change, affected users, consequences, migration/action steps, known limits, and links to durable documentation. Preserve a stable URL/history when it serves users. A truthful date and updated sitemap/IndexNow notification can assist discovery; they do not create freshness or AI visibility on their own. `[G-DATES] [G-HELPFUL] [B-SITEMAPS] [INDEXNOW]`

## Images, video, and interactive assets

Use media because it demonstrates, explains, or gives evidence that text cannot provide as well. Write the necessary surrounding facts and accessible description; do not make an image, a video, or an embed the only location of a claim a visitor needs to assess.

Current secondary research can justify testing a useful video as its own editorial asset in an appropriate market. It does not demonstrate that placing a video or transcript on every landing page increases ranking or citation. `[S-AHREFS-VIDEO]`

For a calculator, mini-app, or data demo, establish ownership before publication: formula/version, data sources, covered inputs and ranges, locale/currency assumptions, how personal or sensitive inputs are handled, and who approves the output claims. This is a content-governance heuristic, not an SEO ranking factor. Publish a stable public destination and a concise explanation of the method/limits only where that is appropriate for visitors.

## Content that fails the standard

Avoid mass-produced thin pages, copied/rephrased pages without new value, forced keyword repetition, fabricated benchmarks, misleading comparisons, fake recency, and attempts to manipulate AI answers. Google and Bing both document quality and spam constraints relevant to these patterns. `[G-ESSENTIALS] [G-AI-OPTIMIZATION] [B-GUIDELINES]`
