# Reference-skill curation

The following skill suites are monitored as **secondary audit inputs**, not authorities. Their useful checklists and linked sources may surface an idea; their rules only enter this skill when the original evidence meets [the evidence policy](evidence-policy.md).

| Source ID | Suite | Use | Do not inherit automatically |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `R-MARKETINGSKILLS` | `coreyhaines31/marketingskills` | Candidate sources and workflow ideas | Unlinked statistics, universal content patterns, platform-specific promises |
| `R-WEB-QUALITY-SKILLS` | `addyosmani/web-quality-skills` | Technical audit patterns | A checklist item as evidence of a ranking factor |
| `R-CODEX-SEO` | `AgriciDaniel/codex-seo` | Candidate technical/content/GEO claims | Internal thresholds, scores, or legacy AI-crawler assertions |
| `R-CLAUDE-SEO` | `AgriciDaniel/claude-seo` | Upstream comparison and its source labeling | Any claim merely because it is labelled as a source or heuristic |

The recurring source watch monitors pinned versions. A maintainer first assesses an update against primary Google/Bing documentation and then decides whether a change belongs in the evidence registry, a test proposal, an exclusion, or nowhere.
